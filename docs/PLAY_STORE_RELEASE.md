## Store listing copy
### Short description
Manage invoices, customers, expenses, payments and business growth with DONIDEX.

### Full description
DONIDEX is a mobile-first business management platform built to help entrepreneurs and small teams run their businesses from one place.

Create professional invoices, quotations and receipts. Manage customers and expenses. Track payments, outstanding balances and overdue invoices. Organize multiple businesses, review reports and business insights, and access growth tools designed to help you stay on top of cash flow and operations.

Key features include:
- Invoices, quotations and receipts
- Customer and expense management
- Payment recording and payment history
- Revenue, outstanding and overdue tracking
- Reports and business insights
- Multi-business management
- Business settings and numbering controls
- Team/staff management for Business plans
- Growth tools for products/services, recurring invoices and payment follow-up
- Secure account and profile controls

DONIDEX is designed for practical day-to-day business management, with a clean mobile-first experience and professional document workflows.
