Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

DONIDEX is a business management platform operated by Doni Studio Production. This policy explains how information is handled when you use DONIDEX.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as email address, username and authentication provider information.
  • Business information and records you choose to store, including customers, invoices, quotations, receipts, expenses, products/services and reports.
  • Profile information you choose to provide, including an avatar or provider profile image.
  • Subscription and payment-related information needed to provide paid features. Payment card details are handled by our payment provider and are not stored by DONIDEX.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide business-management features, synchronize your workspace, process subscriptions, protect accounts, provide support, improve reliability and comply with applicable requirements.

Service providers

DONIDEX may use trusted infrastructure and service providers including Firebase for authentication/database services and Paystack for subscription payment processing. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Data security

We use authentication controls, verified-account gates and access rules intended to keep one user's business data isolated from another user's data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

You may request account/data deletion through the account controls where available or by contacting us. We will process valid deletion requests subject to legal, security and operational requirements.

Your choices

You can update profile and business information in DONIDEX. You can stop using the service and request deletion of your account and associated data.

Children

DONIDEX is intended for business users and is not directed to children.

Contact

For privacy or account questions, contact donidexsupport@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as DONIDEX evolves. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.
